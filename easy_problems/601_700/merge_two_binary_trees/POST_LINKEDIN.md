**Merge Two Binary Trees** (Easy)
🔗 https://leetcode.com/problems/merge-two-binary-trees/

Overlay two trees, sum the overlapping nodes, keep whatever's left when only one side
has a node. The neat trick: you never need to *build* new nodes for the non-overlapping
parts — just hand back whichever tree's subtree is non-nil and reuse it as-is.
