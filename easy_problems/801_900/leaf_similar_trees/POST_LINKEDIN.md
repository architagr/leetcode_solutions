**Leaf-Similar Trees** (Easy)
🔗 https://leetcode.com/problems/leaf-similar-trees/

Two trees can look nothing alike and still count as "leaf-similar." Shape doesn't
matter here, only what you get when you read the leaves left to right. Pull each
tree's leaves into a list and the whole problem turns into a plain list-equality
check.
