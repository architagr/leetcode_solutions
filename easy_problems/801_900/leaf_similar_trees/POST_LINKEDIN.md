**Leaf-Similar Trees** (Easy)
🔗 https://leetcode.com/problems/leaf-similar-trees/

Two trees can look completely different and still be "leaf-similar" — the trick is to
stop comparing shapes altogether. Extract each tree's leaves left-to-right into a
list, then it's just a list-equality check.
