**Sum of Left Leaves** (Easy)
🔗 https://leetcode.com/problems/sum-of-left-leaves/

The catch here: a node can't tell if it's a "left" leaf — only its parent knows which
side it's on. So the check has to happen one level up, from the parent looking down at
its left child, not inside the leaf's own base case.
