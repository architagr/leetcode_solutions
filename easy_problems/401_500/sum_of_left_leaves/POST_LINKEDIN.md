**Sum of Left Leaves** (Easy)
🔗 https://leetcode.com/problems/sum-of-left-leaves/

A node can't tell you if it's a "left" leaf, only its parent knows which side it's
on. So the check has to happen one level up, from the parent looking down at its left
child, not inside the leaf's own base case. Small twist, but it's the whole problem.
