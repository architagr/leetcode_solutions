**Evaluate Boolean Binary Tree** (Easy)
🔗 https://leetcode.com/problems/evaluate-boolean-binary-tree/

The tree *is* the expression: leaves are `True`/`False` literals, internal nodes are
`OR`/`AND` operators waiting on their two children. Post-order recursion evaluates both
children first, then the parent combines them, the same order you'd hand-evaluate
nested parentheses in. Nothing fancy here, just get the order right and the recursion
does the rest.
