**Subtree of Another Tree** (Easy)
🔗 https://leetcode.com/problems/subtree-of-another-tree/

Two problems in a trench coat: "are two trees identical?" plus "try that check at every
node of the bigger tree." The neat trick is checking values first before ever running
the full structural comparison — a cheap filter that skips a lot of wasted recursion.
