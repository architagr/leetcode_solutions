**365 Days of LeetCode Challenge — Day 14/365**

**Subtree of Another Tree** (Easy)
🔗 https://leetcode.com/problems/subtree-of-another-tree/

It's basically two problems wearing a trench coat: check if two trees are identical,
then run that check at every node of the bigger tree. The part I like is the value
check up front, before the full structural comparison ever runs. One comparison, and it
kills a lot of wasted recursion.
