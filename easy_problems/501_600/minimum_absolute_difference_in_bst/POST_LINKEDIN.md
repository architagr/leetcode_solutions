**Minimum Absolute Difference in BST** (Easy)
🔗 https://leetcode.com/problems/minimum-absolute-difference-in-bst/

The trick: an in-order traversal of a BST visits nodes in sorted order, and in a
sorted sequence the smallest gap between any two values is always between
*neighbors*. So there's no need to compare every pair — just track the previous
node visited and compare against it as you go.
