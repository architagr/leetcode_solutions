**Minimum Absolute Difference in BST** (Easy)
🔗 https://leetcode.com/problems/minimum-absolute-difference-in-bst/

The trick: an in-order traversal of a BST hands you the values in sorted order for
free. And in a sorted sequence, the smallest gap between any two values is always
between neighbors, so you never have to compare every pair. Just track the previous
node visited and check against it as you go.
