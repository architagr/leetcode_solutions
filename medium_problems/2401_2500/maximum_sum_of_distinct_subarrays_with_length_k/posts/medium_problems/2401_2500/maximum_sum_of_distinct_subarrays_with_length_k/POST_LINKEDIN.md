Maximum Sum of Distinct Subarrays With Length K (Medium)
🔗 https://leetcode.com/problems/maximum-sum-of-distinct-subarrays-with-length-k/

Two easy problems from the start of this sliding-window run, combined into a medium.

A running sum over a fixed window gives each window's score. A count map that deletes keys
at zero tells you whether the window's values are all different: they are when the map has
k keys.

Carry both, slide once, keep the best sum among the distinct windows.
